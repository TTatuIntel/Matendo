Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I expect to receive matched professionals?
After submitting your request, we typically match you with qualified healthcare professionals within 24-48 hours. For urgent requests, we can expedite the process and provide matches within 24 hours.
What types of healthcare professionals are available?
We offer a wide range of healthcare professionals including physicians, nurses, specialists, medical technicians, therapists, and administrative staff. Our database includes professionals across various specialties and experience levels.
How are the healthcare professionals vetted?
All healthcare professionals in our network undergo a thorough vetting process that includes credential verification, license validation, background checks, reference checks, and skills assessment. We ensure that only qualified professionals are matched with your requests.
What if I'm not satisfied with the matched professionals?
We stand behind our matching process with a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the professionals we've matched, simply let us know and we'll provide alternative matches at no additional cost until you find the right fit for your needs.
Can I request professionals for short-term assignments?
Yes, we offer flexible staffing solutions ranging from single-day assignments to long-term placements. Whether you need coverage for a single shift, temporary leave, or a permanent position, we can accommodate your staffing requirements.
